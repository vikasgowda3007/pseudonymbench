## LLM-Shield-Proxy

It is the most developed benchmarker in the set, and it supports the gap rather
than closing it.

- **It says the gap exists.** `website/docs/split-boundary-leaks.md` line 124:
  "The latency cost of design 2 is unmeasured across implementations ... no one has
  published a comparison of time-to-first-token against an unfiltered stream. That
  number decides whether maintainers accept design 2 or settle for design 1." (Design
  2 is holding back a trailing partial placeholder, which is what this repo does.)
- **Its `benchmarks/REPORTING.md` plans for exactly these measurements** (direct
  mock upstream as the baseline, time-to-first-byte, end-to-end p50/p95/p99/p99.9)
  and its `STABILITY.md` lists long-TTFT behaviour as unverified.
- **Its published HTTP profiles are three-request runs.** The LiteLLM rows
  (`benchmarks/results/http-profile-litellm-1.99.0*.json`) record
  `client_observed_latency` with `iterations: 3`, so p50, p95 and p99 are the same
  number. They are conformance checks that happen to time the call, not a latency
  distribution.
- **Independent evidence about LiteLLM streaming.** Its Presidio row (LiteLLM 1.99.0,
  `mode: pre_call`, `output_parse_pii: true`) reports `response_fidelity` 0 of 3
  and `events_observed: 1`: the original values were not restored to the client and
  the whole reply arrived as one event, which matches the whole-response buffering
  the first research read in LiteLLM's source. It is the project's own result on its
  own harness (Windows, CPython 3.12), not reproduced here.
- Its LiteLLM guardrail configuration (`guardrail: presidio`, `mode: pre_call`,
  `output_parse_pii: true`, `default_on: true`, analyzer and anonymizer base URLs)
  is the same as the template in `bench/rivals/litellm/`.

## k6 and SSE

The spec asked whether k6 can measure SSE time-to-first-token natively. Searching
the k6 documentation, the community extension and the issue tracker found a
community extension, [xk6-sse](https://github.com/phymbert/xk6-sse) (`k6/x/sse`),
and a core feature request, [grafana/k6#746](https://github.com/grafana/k6/issues/746),
closed as not planned; no native SSE module was found. The custom load client stays:
it needs no extension and measures time to the first content delta directly.
